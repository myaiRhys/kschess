# Puddle Jump

A one-button game made with Isla. Open `index.html` in a browser on a laptop and press SPACE.

- The piglet hops into the nearest muddy puddle and counts out loud.
- Press SPACE again while it is in the air to jump higher for a super splash.
- Every 4th puddle is a giant sparkly one: everyone gets muddy and laughs.
- Frogs and ducks sometimes pop out of puddles.
- Every third press the piglet climbs the slide into the big puddle.
- Every 5 splashes a rainbow appears.
