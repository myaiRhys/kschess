# Puddle Jump

A one-button game made with Isla. Open `index.html` in a browser on a laptop and press SPACE.
The piglet hops into the nearest muddy puddle, counts out loud, and every 5 splashes a rainbow appears. Every third press she climbs the slide and whooshes into the big puddle.

After 10 puddles the piglet goes to a giant jungle gym. Use the arrow keys to find the ladders and climb to the flag.
To jump straight to the jungle gym, open the page with `#climb` on the end of the address.
